# The bar: joinhandshake.com (homepage, captured 2026-10-27)

Handshake is the student career network. The user chose it as the reference for our landing page. Below are seven mechanisms that make it read as premium. Each one is checkable by looking at screenshots or a scroll filmstrip.

1. **A poster-scale condensed headline owns the first viewport.**
   - The h1 is a heavy condensed sans in all caps, ~86px on 1440 desktop, roughly 4× the 20px body.
   - Line-height is under 0.85 and tracking is tight, so the two lines lock into one block.
   - It is centred and sits roughly in the upper middle of the viewport.
   - Only two type families appear on the page: the condensed display and one neutral grotesk.

2. **The hero is the product's own input, not a picture of it.**
   - Directly under one short supporting line sits a large, white, rounded search field, ~88px tall and ~920px wide, with a chip row beneath (`AI specialists · Full-time · Remote · Internship`).
   - Its placeholder types itself out character by character, demonstrating a real query.
   - There is no illustration, no device mockup and no stock photo in the first viewport.

3. **A soft colour field glows behind the hero and sinks into a near-black page.**
   - The hero background is a blurred, multi-hue radial field (teal → green, ~2 hues) that darkens at every edge into the page ground, ~#14151A.
   - Below the hero, the page is that single near-black ground: no section changes background colour, and no white bands.
   - The glow returns only inside framed feature panels.

4. **One acid accent, used only on actions and the brand.**
   - A single saturated accent (lime) appears on the primary CTA pills, the logo and the giant footer wordmark, and nowhere else.
   - Body text and cards are white and grey on dark.
   - Secondary buttons are outlined or dark, never a second colour.

5. **Content lives in hairline-bordered dark cards with real specifics.**
   - Opportunity cards are dark (~1–3% lighter than the ground), 1px faint borders, ~10px radius, laid out in a 3-up grid.
   - Each carries concrete data: title, `Up to $80/hr`, and a degree list (`Bachelor's · Master's · Ph.Ds`).
   - Feature rows alternate text-left/panel-right. The panel is a gradient-glow frame holding white UI cards with real names and numbers.

6. **The nav transforms on scroll.**
   - At the top it is a full-width transparent bar (wordmark left, links centred, Log in outline + Sign up accent at the right).
   - After the hero it becomes a compact floating white pill, centred at ~520px wide, with the logo shrunk to a monogram.
   - The change is a single smooth transition, not a jump.

7. **The page closes on a giant wordmark.**
   - The footer is link columns, then the brand name set in the accent colour at full container width (~200px tall).
   - It is the largest type on the page and ends the scroll like a signature.
   - Motion everywhere is calm. Things fade or slide a short distance once. Nothing loops except the placeholder typing and the logo marquee.

## How the critics use this

The craft critic puts screenshots of ours (desktop 1440 at several scroll offsets, plus mobile 390) next to `handshake/*.png` with labels stripped. It says which page is better on these seven points and names the single biggest gap. We win when a student would pick ours as the more premium product. Copying Handshake's colours, words or wordmark loses automatically: ours must be recognisably Beyond the Rumour Mill (see `design-system.md`).
